🎲 Ludo Rules
✕

• Starting: Roll a 6 to move a token out of your home base onto the track.


• Extra Rolls: Rolling a 6 or capturing an opponent grants another turn!


• Capturing: Land on an opponent's token to send it back to their base.


• Safe Stars: Tokens on squares marked with ★ cannot be captured.


• Winning: First player to lead all 4 tokens into the center home triangle wins the match!
